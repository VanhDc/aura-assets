# Ravine — asset credits

Original terrarium composition, enclosure, botanical details, lighting, assembly choreography and website are part of this project.

Original humidity details: the meniscus droplet mesh and lightweight geometry variant were modeled procedurally in Blender for this project. Editable source: `humidity-details.blend`, generated by `create_humidity_asset.py`. Dew placement, wet-cuticle shading, condensation, drip choreography and mist/light timing are project-authored.

## Runtime

Three.js r180, MIT license. Includes OrbitControls, GLTFLoader, Meshopt decoding, and the Water2 reflection/refraction implementation. The distribution includes THREE-LICENSE.txt.

This is a digital ecosystem composition, not an animal-stocking guide.

## Anatomical beetles and editorial imagery

The ground beetle geometry, chitin textures, joint hierarchy and stationary idle animation were created in Blender for this revision. The interactive macro and shoreline LOD retain the same authored 14-second loop. Anatomy references and native source are in the revision’s `revisions/insects` folder.

The editorial scene plates and close-ups are renders of this project’s actual geometry. Page layout, lighting dial and scroll interactions are project-authored.
